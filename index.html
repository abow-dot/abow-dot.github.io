About ARTickles

ARTickles is a space where graphical art and poetry coexist — not as separate disciplines, but as two ways of seeing the same world. Each piece here began as an impulse: something noticed, felt, or imagined that demanded to be made visible.

Whether through line, colour, or carefully chosen words, the work here is an ongoing exploration of what images and language can do that neither can manage alone.

About ShuPri

ShuPri is a writer, artist and poet based in Bangalore, India. Following a long career in advertising as a Creative Director, ShuPri now creates work that bridges the gap between the visual and the written, exploring everyday items with serendipity and humour, transforming the ordinary into extraordinary.
